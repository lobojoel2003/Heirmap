# Code of Conduct

Heirmap serves families dealing with loss, land and money, often across generations of hardship.
We expect everyone in this project to act with the same care.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who are not programmers or lawyers.
- Never share real family records, names or case details in issues or pull requests. Use fictional
  examples.
- Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation to keep the community
safe. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
